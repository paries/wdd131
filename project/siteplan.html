Palms Picklers

Site Name

Palms Picklers - Is an existing club in the Palms RV Resort in Yuma Az. Possible domain : palmspicklers.org

Site Purpose

The page will provide information about the Club, some photos, contact information and the abilty to organize a Round Robin event by collecting participants and generating game schedule.

Scenarios

  • What is the Palms Picklers
  • How can I contact the organizers
  • I want to organize a Round Robin event.
